Online Donations to
Ramakrishna Math and
Ramakrishna Mission, Belur Math
The Ramakrishna Math and the Ramakrishna Mission serve the needy through various service activities, as chalked out by Swami Vivekananda. Admirers and devotees, who are in sympathy with the objects of the twin organizations are cordially invited to help us by contributing to one or more of the funds, which need their active support.
Donations to the Ramakrishna Math and the Ramakrishna Mission
made through any Electronic Modeare exempt from Income Tax underClause (ii) of 2nd proviso to Section 80G (5) of IT Act 1961.
Exemption Limit: 50% of the donation amount or 10% of the Annual Adjusted Gross Total Income of the donor, whichever is lower. For Form 10BE Certificate of any financial year, please write to us, if not received before 31 May of the succeeding year.
To avail 80G tax exemption, PAN is mandatory.
